01 Brand

The diya that says done

A diya is lit at almost every Indian celebration: weddings, birthdays, Diwali, griha pravesh. Utsav's mark is a clay diya seen from the side, in a rounded Aubergine tile. The bowl tapers into a pronounced spout on the right, a short wick stands in the spout, and the Marigold flame rises from that wick in an S-curve, carrying a check mark. Celebration and planning are one shape: every task you finish keeps the lamp lit. Three shapes only (tile, bowl with spout and wick, flame with tick), so it holds up at 16 px. No sparkles, no confetti.

logo.svg · mark + custom monoline wordmark drawn as strokes (no font dependency). The "t" carries a small Marigold flame instead of a plain ascender: the one ownable detail of the wordmark.

Rules

  • Clear space = flame height (25% of the tile) on every side. Minimum: mark 16 px, lockup 96 px wide.
  • Flame is always Marigold (or currentColor in mono). Never separate the tick from the flame.
  • No gradients, shadows, outlines, rotation or sparkle add-ons. Never on a Marigold background.
  • The mark keeps the same colours in both themes: plum tile, Ivory bowl, Marigold flame (6.29:1 on the tile). On dark surfaces the tile gets a 1.5-unit #D8B4E2 ring so its edge holds against the dark canvas. Only the wordmark strokes theme. The favicon uses the same values.
  • Wordmark: round-capped 6-unit monoline strokes; the "t" ascender is replaced by a small flame (Marigold in colour, currentColor in mono). role="img", aria-label="Utsav".
  • The flame always sits on the wick at the spout (right of centre) and leans back over the bowl. Never centre it or mirror the mark.

Motifs (sparingly)

Toran edge: landing hero, onboarding Done card, share page header only. Rangoli dot grid: behind empty states and the generating card.

Keyboard flow: reviewing AI suggestions

  1. Ctrl/⌘ + K opens AI mode and focuses the instruction box (aria-keyshortcuts). No single-key shortcuts exist.
  2. Ctrl/⌘ + Enter submits, or Tab to Suggest. Panel order: Close → instruction → Suggest → example chips → Waiting for you → earlier turns → batch.
  3. While waiting, the staged progress card is announced politely; Cancel is reachable.
  4. On arrival, focus moves to the batch heading (h2, tabindex -1) unless you moved focus elsewhere; "3 suggestions, 2 valid" is announced.
  5. Tab: Accept all valid → card 1 Accept → Edit → Decline → inline control → card 2 …
  6. Accept or Decline collapses the card and focuses its Undo or Restore. Restore returns focus to Accept.
  7. Edit focuses the first field; Esc or Cancel returns to Edit; Save and accept lands on Undo.
  8. Accept all focuses the summary line ("2 applied, 1 skipped: …") with batch Undo. The toast never steals focus; Esc dismisses it. Code proposal cards created inline (Add to plan, Reset section) follow the same order in place.
  9. Closing the panel returns focus to the AI mode button. On mobile the sheet traps focus and closes with Esc.

Privacy and AI

Plain answers to what Utsav keeps, what it sends to Google Gemini, and how to delete it.

What we store

Your Google name, photo and email (from sign-in), your profile answers, and your events: budget, timeline, vendor checklist, ideas and guest counts. Guest counts only, never guest names or phone numbers. Vendor labels and contacts you type are visible only to you.

What we send to Gemini

Event type, occasion and milestone, date, city, guest counts, budget lines, functions, tasks, vendor categories and statuses, tradition, dietary needs, and honorees' roles, ages and interests. Never names, private notes, event titles, vendor labels or contacts. Calls go through Vertex AI (global endpoint); your data is stored in Mumbai (asia-south1).

What we never do

Sell your data, send messages for you, or list or recommend specific businesses. Costs are rough estimates, not quotes. AI cost per plan in the README is an estimate.

Delete your data

You can delete any event and everything in it from its Event settings. To delete your whole account, email privacy@utsav.example and we will do it within 30 days.

When F-20 ships (P1), the second sentence becomes "Delete your account in Settings" with a link.
